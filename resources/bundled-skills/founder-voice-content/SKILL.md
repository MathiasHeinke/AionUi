# Founder Voice → Content

The operator's lived client experience is the only content that **can't be faked** — and the only content an AI ruins by
making it sound like every other AI. The job is two moves: capture **how the operator actually sounds** once, then let any
client moment flow out *in that voice*. **Show the lesson, don't claim the expertise.** A post that sounds like him and
tells one true moment beats a polished post that sounds like a LinkedIn-bot. Specificity is the trust signal; the
operator's real cadence is the moat no competitor can copy.

## When to use
- The operator brings a real client moment — "ich hatte grad das mit einem Kunden", "this just happened" — and wants it
  turned into content: "mach 'nen Beitrag draus", "make a post", "schreib das für LinkedIn / als Blog".
- "Mach es so, dass es nach mir klingt" / "in my voice" — or first-time voice capture / "refresh my voice profile".
- NOT for cold outreach (use **marketing-outbound**) and NOT for a known-ICP sales/landing page (use **landing-copy**).
  This is the operator's OWN inbound personal-brand voice, fed by his lived experience.

## The method
Two phases. Capture the voice once; then any anecdote is a fast draft. Never skip the anonymization step.

### Phase A — Capture / refresh the Founder Voice (once, then on request)
1. **Gather signal.** Ingest 3–5 real samples (past posts, or how he talks in this chat). If none exist, interview with
   five sharp questions: the register (Du/Sie, DE/EN), the words he will NOT use, his recurring phrases, the 2–3 themes he
   owns, and one post he wishes he'd written + why.
2. **Distill a Voice Fingerprint** — not a vibe, a usable spec: register + language · sentence rhythm (short/punchy vs.
   layered) · signature phrases · a vocabulary **do / don't** list · emoji + hashtag stance · the 3 themes he owns ·
   one-line "sounds-like-him" test.
3. **Persist it.** Write the fingerprint to **USER.md** under a `## Founder Voice` block — it auto-loads every turn and
   stays on the local lane (never egresses). Confirm with the operator before you save profile facts. Keep long verbatim
   samples short here; if they bloat context, keep only the distilled fingerprint and note where the raw samples live.

### Phase B — Anecdote → drafts
4. **Take the story** (spoken or typed) and extract the **ONE transferable lesson**, not the play-by-play. What can a
   reader who wasn't there *use*?
5. **Anonymize — non-negotiable.** Never name, brand, or expose a client; strip identifying detail (invisible-delivery,
   eve-doctrine). The lesson is the asset, the client's identity never is.
6. **Pick channels** with the operator: LinkedIn post · short-form hook/script (X / IG / Reel) · blog outline→draft ·
   newsletter blurb. Default to LinkedIn + a blog outline unless he says otherwise.
7. **Draft each in the Voice Fingerprint**, reusing the craft: landing-copy's *show-the-result-with-a-real-number* and a
   forced-yes opener; marketing-outbound's tight first line. Read the USER.md voice block back as the binding constraint —
   if a draft would pass as generic AI, it failed; rewrite until it sounds like him.
8. **Self-check** each draft against the do/don't list, and flag any claim that isn't true to his lived experience as a
   hypothesis to verify before it goes out.

## Output
- (Phase A) the **Voice Fingerprint** saved to USER.md + a one-line confirmation of what was captured.
- (Phase B) per chosen channel: a **ready-to-post draft**, a one-line "why this hook", and the anonymization note —
  followed by a single **post / revise / discard** decision per draft (the operator's call, never auto-sent).

## Key discipline
- **His voice, not EVE's.** If a draft would pass as generic AI, it failed — rewrite until it sounds like him.
- **Show the lesson, don't claim expertise.** One concrete moment beats five adjectives.
- **Never name the client.** Invisible-delivery is non-negotiable — the lesson travels, the client's identity never does.
- **Draft, never publish.** No post leaves without the operator's explicit approval; there is no auto-send.

## For Command EVE
This is the real engine behind the dead "content-machine" label — a native EVE capability, not a department promise.

- **Capture once, reuse forever.** EVE learns Alois's voice ONE time into USER.md; from then on, any time he tells EVE
  about a client moment — by voice (local STT) or text — EVE hands back posts that sound like him, anonymized and gated,
  his to approve.
- **Voice stays local.** The Founder Voice profile lives in USER.md on the operator's machine and never egresses — the
  personal-brand fingerprint is exactly the kind of data that must not leave the Mac.
- **Then Alois for his clients.** The same skill, pointed at a *client's* brand voice, becomes the operator's content
  engine for each client — each client's voice profile and material isolated, the work delivered under the client's name,
  never EVE's (invisible-delivery, per-client isolation).
- **Evidence rule.** Every claim in a draft must trace to something the operator actually lived; an unverifiable claim is
  flagged as a hypothesis before it can be posted — the operator's approval is the real gate.
