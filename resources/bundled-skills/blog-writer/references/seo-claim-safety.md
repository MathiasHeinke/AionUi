# Blog Writer SEO And Claim Safety

## SEO Scaffold

For search-intent pieces, create:

- Title tag: 60 characters or less, primary keyword near the front, human-readable.
- Meta description: 155 characters or less, with a concrete reason to click.
- URL slug: short, readable, lowercase, keyword-aligned.
- Primary keyword: used naturally 3-5 times, with real synonyms instead of stuffing.
- Internal links: 2-3 marked slots like `[link: related guide]`.
- H-structure: H2/H3 headings that let a scanner understand the argument without reading every paragraph.

Readability beats density. If SEO pressure makes the post sound generic, revise the scaffold rather than damaging the voice.

## Claim-Safety Pass

Before returning the draft, check for:

- Unverifiable superlatives such as "the best", "#1" or "guaranteed" without proof.
- Invented testimonials, case studies, numbers, screenshots or quotes.
- Health, financial, legal or regulated-industry promises.
- Competitor disparagement or claims that need side-by-side evidence.
- Personal data that is not approved for publication.
- German audience surfaces that could trigger UWG or DSGVO risk.

For any flagged item, either ground it with a real source, soften it, cut it, or mark the required operator decision. Never silently ship the risky version.
